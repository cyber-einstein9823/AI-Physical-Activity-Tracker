Analysis

  • Walking, upstairs, downstairs and laying are recognised at 97.6–99.8% — their motion (or gravity direction) is highly distinctive.
  • Sitting (81.9%) and standing (84.2%) are the weak spot: both are nearly motionless, so the signal differences are subtle and depend on posture and phone orientation.
  • Attention is sharply peaked for dynamic activities (mean max weight ≈ 0.21–0.27) but almost flat for static ones (≈ 0.01), which fits the intuition that static windows have no “key moment”.
  • Limitations: single dataset, fixed phone placement, educational use only — not for medical or safety-critical decisions.

What happened

Training loss fell quickly and plateaued. Validation loss bottomed out at epoch 18 (0.0765) and then crept up while training loss stayed low — classic mild overfitting — so the checkpoint was chosen by validation loss, not the last epoch. Peak validation accuracy (~98.1%) happened earlier, at epoch 3, which is why loss and accuracy disagree about the “best” epoch.
